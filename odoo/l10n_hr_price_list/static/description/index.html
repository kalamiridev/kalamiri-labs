Public Price List for Consumers

Croatia · Rulebooks NN 132/26 · Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise

Publishes the machine-readable price lists of goods and services (CSV) that Croatian shops, service providers and web shops with a website must publish for every premises, with the base price next to every price.

Hrvatski: javni cjenici robe i usluga za potrošače u CSV datotekama za svaki objekt (prodavaonica, ured, salon, web shop) ili za sva prodajna mjesta, s baznom cijenom, propisanim stupcima i nazivima datoteka, arhivom, akcijama iz cjenika i webhookom za vašu mrežnu stranicu (Pravilnici NN 132/26, od 17. 11. 2026.). Upute na hrvatskom su u README-u modula.

What it does

  • Goods and services price lists per premises – shop, office, salon or web shop – or one for all premises, with the prescribed columns and file names (OIB, premises, ROBA/USLUGE, storage number, time).
  • Consumer prices as on a quotation: the default pricelist of the company (or the sales price) with the taxes of the product. Nothing to set up on the premises.
  • Base price (price on 2 May 2025 or 10 September 2026, or at the first publication of a product) saved with its date and never changed by later prices. Administrators can correct it.
  • Special sales are ordinary pricelist rules (discount, validity period) with the name of the sale: the price list marks them automatically and goes back to the regular price when they end.
  • Always up to date: a new version a minute after a change, and every morning for the start and end of special sales (the deadline is 11:00).
  • Public links without login: the current file always on the same link (/cjenik/<label>/roba, /usluge), replaced versions in the archive.
  • Optional webhook to your own website (JSON, HMAC-SHA256 signature), with the price before a sale and the lowest price in the 30 days before it.

Premises

Invoicing (Accounting) → Configuration → Public Price List Premises. Product categories are optional: empty publishes all sellable products.

Special sales: the Odoo way

Add a rule to the default pricelist (Sales → Products → Pricelists): a discount for a product or a category, a validity period and the name of the special sale. The same rule applies on quotations, so the published price is the price on the invoice.

Products

The brand and the base prices of each premises, on the product form.

Versions

Every version with its price list (goods or services), storage number, file and webhook state.

The files

CSV, UTF-8 with BOM, semicolons, decimal comma; the prescribed columns in the prescribed order.

Goods: naziv; sifra; marka; neto_kolicina; jedinica_mjere; maloprodajna_cijena_eur; posebni_oblik_prodaje; cijena_za_jedinicu_mjere_eur; barkod; ean_barkod; bazna_cijena_eur

Services: naziv; maloprodajna_cijena_eur; posebni_oblik_prodaje; bazna_cijena_eur

Limitations

  • One price for all premises (the default pricelist); premises with their own prices are not supported.
  • Promotions of Discount & Loyalty (e.g. 2+1) are conditions on an order, not product prices, and are not shown.
  • The lowest price in the 30 days before a sale is sent on the webhook; showing it next to the sale price is up to you.
